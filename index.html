Training is a long journey. It takes organisation, dedication and persistence: in your plan, your food and your sleep. You don't compare yourself with anyone. Only with who you were yesterday.

What it does

A coach that reads your training, not your mood.

A plan built from the research

  • For each muscle, the plan sets how many sets you do in a week, within the ranges the research supports. If you are new, you start with fewer.
  • Heavy lifts come first. When you train four or more days a week, a session stays at 24 sets or fewer, warm-up sets not counted.
  • Each day shows how long the workout will take, with rests and warm-up included.
Apple Watch

Leave the phone in your bag.

Log the set from your wrist. The watch taps you when the rest is over.

  • Sets. Weight and reps come filled in from last time. Turn the Crown, tap Log set.
  • Rest. The ring counts down and your wrist buzzes at zero.
  • Cardio. Time against your target, and your heart rate against its zone.
  • Finish. The watch keeps recording until you save the workout on your iPhone.

Phone out of reach? Sets are kept on the watch and delivered when it is back. Apple Watch is free until Pro launches.

Questions people ask.

Is Kompsatos free?

Yes. The coach is free, always: logging, plans, load suggestions, records, guidance, export of your data and the rest timer on your Lock Screen. A paid Pro tier is planned for extras such as full chart history (Free shows 3 months), Apple Watch, widgets and themes, at a planned €3.99 a month or €29.99 a year. Prices may change before launch.

When do I get access to the beta?

Right away. On iPhone, open the beta link, install Apple's TestFlight app if it asks, then tap Install. On Android it takes two steps, on the Android page. Places on iPhone are limited; if the beta is full, join the waitlist and you will hear from us when places open.

Do I need an account, and where does my data go?

No account is needed. As a guest everything stays on your phone. With an account, and only with your explicit consent, your training and your settings are backed up to a database in Frankfurt, Germany, so you can restore them on another phone. No ads, no analytics, and your data is never sold. Details in the privacy policy.

Does it work without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Everything works on the phone alone, on iPhone and on Android. The watch is an extra for logging from your wrist.

How does a physiotherapist help?

As a physiotherapist, I read the training research and turn it into the app: how many sets, how much weight, how much rest, which mistakes to avoid. If you have a question about the theory behind a rule, write to me. Pain or an injury needs an in-person assessment by a doctor or a physiotherapist.
