What it does

A coach that reads your training, not your mood.

Training

Plans that know your week

Log every set with weight, reps and reps in reserve. Your next load comes from your own recent performance.

Records

Records that are real

Estimated maxes and personal records are counted per exercise and per machine, so a lighter cable stack never fakes a record.

Recovery & nutrition

The rest of your week, in context

Sleep, body weight and waist trends sit next to your training, with protein and rate-of-loss guidance taken from meta-analyses.

Progress

Rewards for consistency, not for overdoing it

172 achievements, 100 levels, titles and colours you unlock. Points come from keeping your plan, never from extra volume.

Evidence first

The numbers come with sources.

A few of the rules inside the app, and where they come from.

1.6–2.2 g/kg

Daily protein beyond which extra protein added little to muscle gain in adults doing resistance training.

Morton RW et al. Br J Sports Med 2018;52:376–384 (meta-analysis, 49 trials).
0.5–1% / week

Rate of body-weight loss recommended to keep muscle during a cut.

Helms ER, Aragon AA, Fitschen PJ. J Int Soc Sports Nutr 2014;11:20.
809,986 entries

Tested powerlifting results behind the strength levels you can unlock for squat, bench press and deadlift.

van den Hoek DJ et al. J Sci Med Sport 2024;27(10):734–742.
≤ 10 reps

Estimated maxes are trusted up to 10 reps to failure. Longer sets count only as a lower bound, because predictions get less accurate as reps go up.

Reynolds JM, Gordon TJ, Robergs RA. J Strength Cond Res 2006;20(3):584–592.
