The hard part isn't logging your sets.

It's knowing what to do next. Kompsatos reads what you actually did last session and gives you one target for today — the weight, the reps, and the reason it changed. On your phone, without an account, without the internet.

Most training apps are spreadsheets with rounded corners.

They are very good at storing what you lifted. They are silent about the only question that matters when you are standing in front of the rack.

01

They record. They don't decide.

Logging is the easy half. Turning eight weeks of sets into "put 42.5 on the bar and stop at seven" is the half that changes results — and it is the half almost every app leaves to you.

02

They ignore what just happened.

A program written in January doesn't know that last Tuesday you left three reps in reserve and the bar moved fast. Kompsatos derives today's target from your last real performance on that exact exercise.

03

They want your health data.

Injuries, sleep, medical notes — uploaded by default, to someone's server, for reasons that serve the company. In Kompsatos that category of data does not leave your phone at all.

What it actually does

No promises about features that don't exist yet. This is what runs today.

  • → One target per exercise, with the reason shown Weight, rep range, and the change from last time — plus why it moved. You are never asked to trust a number you cannot interrogate.
  • → Your last session, on screen, while you lift The sets you actually completed for this exercise sit directly above today's input, so the comparison is immediate rather than remembered.
  • → Progression that respects proximity to failure Reps in reserve are recorded per set and feed the next recommendation, instead of being decoration in a notes field.
  • → Set-by-set logging built for a gym, not a desk Steppers on weight, reps and RIR. A rest timer that starts itself. Nothing that requires two clean hands and full attention.
  • → Programs, cardio, body measurements, streaks The whole training record in one place, so the coaching layer has something real to reason about.
  • → Works with no account and no signal Open it in a basement gym on airplane mode and everything works. Sync exists, is optional, and only ever runs when you tap it.

Who builds it

I'm Dimitrios Lazaridis, a physiotherapist in Heraklion, Crete. I work in neurological rehabilitation, and I supervise clinical interns and teach student practitioners.

Most of my working life is spent on one specific gap: the distance between what the evidence actually supports and what people end up doing in a gym or a clinic. That gap is where injuries and wasted years live. It is also, more or less exactly, what this app is trying to close.

Kompsatos is not a startup pitch. It's the tool I wanted to exist, built to a standard I'd be willing to hand to a patient — which is a much higher bar than the one most fitness software is built to. I train with it myself, and every decision it makes is one I have to live with under a loaded bar.

Where your data lives

Short version, in plain language. The full policy is one click away.

Use it without an account and nothing leaves your phone. No sign-in, no anonymous identity created in the background, no network calls. Your training history is a database file on your device.

Health, injury, sleep and recovery notes stay on the device in every case — even if you do create an account and sync. That path is closed in this build, deliberately, and reopening it would require changing the published privacy declaration first.

No crash reporting. No advertising. No analytics SDK. Errors stay on the device.
