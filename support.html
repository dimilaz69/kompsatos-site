Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: hello@kompsatos.com. We usually answer within two working days.

How do I join the beta?

Fill in the beta form. Testers are invited in small waves. When it is your turn you get an email from Apple's TestFlight with a link to install the app.

Which phones are supported?

iPhone only for now. Android users can join the waitlist through the same form.

How do I send feedback during the beta?

Take a screenshot inside the app and TestFlight will offer to send it to us with a note. You can also email us.

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest everything stays on your phone. An account lets you back up your data and restore it on a new phone.

How do I export or delete my data?

Export: Settings → Data & Backup → Export, as a CSV or JSON file.

Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account. You confirm with your password and by typing DELETE. This permanently deletes your account and all data synced to it from our servers. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app, so export first if you want a copy. As a guest, deleting the app deletes everything.
