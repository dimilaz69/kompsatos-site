Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Applies to the Kompsatos iOS app and this website

The short version. Kompsatos is offline-first. If you use it without creating an account, nothing you enter ever leaves your phone. If you do create an account, training data is uploaded only when you tap sync — never in the background. Health, injury, sleep and recovery notes stay on your device in all cases. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporting.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Dimitrios Lazaridis, an individual developer based in Greece, operating the Kompsatos app and this website.

Contact for privacy matters: privacy@kompsatos.com. Written enquiries can be addressed to that mailbox and will receive a reply from the controller directly.

2. Data stored on your device

By default, everything you enter stays in a database file inside the app's private storage on your phone. This includes:

  • Workout sessions, individual sets, weights, repetitions and reps-in-reserve
  • Cardio sessions, including duration, distance and average heart rate where you enter it
  • Body measurements — weight, body fat and girth measurements
  • Training programs and templates
  • App settings, achievements and streaks
  • Your local athlete profile, including any health, injury, sleep, nutrition and recovery notes you choose to record

You can produce a local file export of your training data at any time from Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data permanently.

3. What can leave your device, and when

Nothing is transmitted unless you both (a) create an account and sign in, and (b) actively tap sync. Sync does not run in the background, on a schedule, or on app launch.

CategoryUploaded on manual sync?
Workouts, sessions, exercises, sets, cardio (incl. average heart rate)Yes
Body measurements — weight, body fat, girthsYes
Training programs and coach summariesYes
Account email, display name and user IDYes — required to hold an account
Health, injury, sleep, nutrition and recovery notesNo — never

Uploaded data is stored under a record keyed to your own user ID and is readable only by your account. Access rules deny everything by default.

4. Health-related data

The app lets you record notes that qualify as special category data under Article 9 GDPR — medical restrictions, injury history, sleep and recovery. In this version, that data is not transmitted anywhere. The upload path for it is disabled in the shipped build.

If that ever changes, it will require your explicit, separate consent, and this policy and the app's published privacy declaration will be updated before any such feature is released. The measurements listed in the table above (weight, body fat, girths, heart rate) are treated as fitness data and are uploaded only on manual sync as described.

5. What Kompsatos does not do

  • No crash reporting. Errors are handled on the device and are not sent anywhere.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK. Your activity is not measured, profiled or shared.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Who processes data on our behalf

When you use an account and sync, two services operated by Google are used as processors:

  • Firebase Authentication — to hold your sign-in credentials and identify your account
  • Cloud Firestore — to store the synced training records listed above

International transfers. Google is a processor acting on documented instructions under the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Depending on the region the database is hosted in, your synced records may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which form part of that addendum.

If you use the app without an account, none of this applies to you, because no data reaches Google at all.

7. Legal bases for processing

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — holding your account and syncing your training records when you ask the app to.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for anything optional you opt into. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

Using the app without an account involves no processing by us at all, because no data reaches us.

8. How long data is kept

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Synced data is kept in your account for as long as the account exists. When an account is deleted, the records stored under it are deleted with it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to processing, and receive a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these, write to privacy@kompsatos.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Greece this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα), www.dpa.gr.

10. Deleting your data

  • Local data: uninstall the app, which removes it permanently from your device.
  • Account and synced data: open Settings, choose your account, and use Delete account. You will be asked for your password and to type DELETE, because the action cannot be undone. Everything stored under your account is removed first, and the account itself is deleted only once nothing remains. You can also write to privacy@kompsatos.com; requests are actioned without undue delay and in any case within one month.

Deleting your account does not erase the training history held on your own phone. Use Settings → Export training data first if you want a copy, and uninstall the app to remove the local database.

11. Children

Kompsatos is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16. No account should be created for a child.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what happens to your data, the date at the top will change and the updated policy will be published here before the change takes effect in the app.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@kompsatos.com
General support: support@kompsatos.com

This policy describes the behaviour of the current build of the app. It is written in plain language deliberately; where it is more specific than a typical policy, that is because the underlying behaviour is verifiable in the app itself.
