Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Kompsatos collects, why, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It covers this website, the beta sign-up form and the Kompsatos iPhone app.

Who is responsible

Dimitris Lazaridis, Heraklion, Crete, Greece, is the controller of your personal data. Contact: hello@kompsatos.com.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it loads no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub, Inc. may process your IP address in server logs to deliver and secure the site.

The beta sign-up form

What we collectYour email address and, if you give them, your first name, the phone you use (iPhone or Android), your country, your training experience and goal, and how you heard about us.
WhyTo invite you to the beta and to send you messages about it.
Legal basisYour consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us; we then delete your entry.

If you are invited, Apple sends the invitation through TestFlight and processes your data under its own privacy policy. Through TestFlight we see the name and email you use there, your device model and iOS version, crash information Apple collects for beta builds, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send.

The Kompsatos app

Without an account

As a guest, everything you log stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

When you create an account, the app asks for your explicit consent before anything leaves your phone. With your consent, the app keeps a copy of your training in your account so you can restore it on another phone. When you delete an entry in the app, its content is removed from that copy too; only a marker with its ID, dates and a sync version stays, so your other phones know it was deleted. It syncs while the app is open: after you save something and when you open the app. It never runs in the background. When you agree, you also confirm that you are 18 or older. We keep a record of your consent (policy version, date and app version) in your account, so we can show that you agreed; it is deleted together with your cloud copy.

What is synced:

  • workouts, sessions, exercises and sets, and cardio including average heart rate;
  • body measurements (body weight, body fat, girths such as waist);
  • training programs, the names you give your gym machines, and the app's coaching summaries;
  • your account email, display name and a user ID.

What is never synced: your sleep log and your Athlete Twin profile (medical restrictions, injury history and similar notes). They stay on your phone.

Training, heart-rate and body-measurement data can be health data under GDPR Art. 9. We process it only to give you the app's features, on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a)). We never use it for advertising, never sell it and never share it with anyone else. Only your account can read it.

Where it is stored

Training and body dataGoogle Cloud Firestore, database located in Frankfurt, Germany (europe-west3).
Account (email, password, sign-in)Firebase Authentication, run by Google from data centres in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified, and Google's data processing terms.

Crash reports

If you are signed in and you turn on “Send crash reports”, the app sends a technical report when it fails, through Firebase Crashlytics (Google). A report contains the error, the code path where it happened, your device model and iOS version, and installation identifiers. It never contains your training, health or injury data. Crash reports are off unless you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings. Google keeps them for 90 days and may process them outside the EU under the safeguards above.

The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

Your rights and controls

  • Export: Settings → Data & Backup → Export training data, as a CSV or JSON file.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all data synced to it from our servers. Google removes remaining copies from its backups within 180 days. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Cloud backup in Settings → Data & Backup. The app stops syncing and deletes the copy in your account from our servers. Data on your phone stays. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.
  • You can also ask us for a copy of your data, its correction, its deletion or its transfer by emailing hello@kompsatos.com; we answer within one month.
  • You can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Age

Kompsatos is intended for adults (18 and over).

Not medical advice

Kompsatos provides training information based on published research. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have a medical condition, pain or an injury, talk to a qualified health professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. If a change affects data you have already given us, we tell you before it applies.
