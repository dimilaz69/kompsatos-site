Evidence first

The numbers come with sources.

Some of the rules inside the app, where each one comes from, and a tool to try.

Seven rules, seven sources.

Open any of them and read it yourself.

67 studies

More weekly sets build more muscle, with diminishing returns. Your weekly sets per muscle are set within that dose-response, and lower when you start out.

Pelland JC et al. Sports Med 2026;56(2):481–505. Meta-regression, 2,058 participants.doi:10.1007/s40279-025-02344-w
≈ 11 sets

Per muscle in one session, gains levelled off at about this point. The app aims to stay below it and spreads the rest over the week.

Remmert JF et al. SportRxiv 2025, preprint. Meta-regression of per-session volume.
Heavy lifts first

Strength rose most in the exercises done first in a session. So a long day trims the arms, not the squat.

Nunes JP et al. Eur J Sport Sci 2021;21(2):149–157. Meta-analysis.doi:10.1080/17461391.2020.1733672
1.6–2.2 g/kg

Daily protein beyond which extra protein added little to muscle gain in adults doing resistance training.

Morton RW et al. Br J Sports Med 2018;52:376–384. Meta-analysis, 49 trials.doi:10.1136/bjsports-2017-097608
0.5–1% / week

Rate of body-weight loss recommended to keep muscle during a cut.

Helms ER, Aragon AA, Fitschen PJ. J Int Soc Sports Nutr 2014;11:20.doi:10.1186/1550-2783-11-20
809,986 entries

Tested powerlifting results behind the strength levels you can unlock for squat, bench press and deadlift.

van den Hoek DJ et al. J Sci Med Sport 2024;27(10):734–742.doi:10.1016/j.jsams.2024.07.005
≤ 10 reps

Estimated maxes are trusted up to 10 reps to failure. Longer sets count only as a lower bound.

Reynolds JM, Gordon TJ, Robergs RA. J Strength Cond Res 2006;20(3):584–592.doi:10.1519/R-15304.1

How to read these numbers

Marks are the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile of lift ÷ body weight among drug-tested, unequipped powerlifters aged 18–35, the same numbers the app uses. The estimated max uses the Epley equation, which is trusted up to 10 reps to failure. Nothing you type here is stored or sent anywhere.
